Party standing-art scale correction · test.8 candidate

Changed production scope
  app.js: new pure setupArtLayout helper and drawSetupArt only.
  Original artwork, battle rendering, combat data and CSS are unchanged by this change.

Diagnosis and measurement
  Old drawSetupArt used min(canvasWidth / PNG width, canvasHeight / PNG height).
  On a 280 × 380 party canvas, the same rule produced these standing-reference heights:
    Amy human       359 × (280 / 418)       = 240.478 px
    Lynae normal    350 × (280 / 299)       = 327.759 px
    Mornye normal   358 × (280 / 427)       = 234.754 px
    Rover female    349.977 × (280 / 448)   = 218.736 px
  Lynae was therefore 1.498 times the female Rover's reference-body height.
  These are manifest-based body measurements, not guessed visible-silhouette heights.

New layout rule
  The common standing-reference target is min(canvasHeight × .70, canvasWidth × .86).
  At the authored 280 × 380 size, every existing idle form has 240.8 px reference body height.
  Ground anchors land at canvasHeight × .90, or y = 342 px at that size.
  Body metadata precedence: referenceBodyHeight, standingBodyHeight, bodyHeight,
  body_reference_height, then a defensive head-to-ground fallback only for missing metadata.
  Pixel and normalized anchors are both supported. Source-dimension changes rescale metadata.
  alphaBBox is used solely for a 4% safe margin and horizontal containment; equipment,
  long hair and transparent padding do not define the body-height ruler. Unusually large
  future/imported silhouettes can uniformly shrink as a last safety constraint.
  Authored feet/ground are respected. Pixels below them, such as Chisa's scissors, remain
  visible; a weapon tip is not mistaken for a foot or forced onto the floor.
  CreationArt.render().meta is retained, including the custom standing height and ground.
  Portraits keep their existing independent crop and manual portrait calibration.
  Canvas imageSmoothingEnabled remains false; stale load/error/custom promises and detached
  canvases cannot repaint after a newer request.
